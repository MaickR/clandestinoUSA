Our Flagship Wine

Pure Soul

NTC & Sons · Barrel Aged · 2023

Born at 600–850 meters in D.O.P. Cariñena, northeast Spain, from bush-trained, dry-farmed Garnacha vines over 40 years old. Hand-harvested and aged eight months on its lees, Pure Soul is savory, fleshy and full of personality, released in a limited edition of 3,000 bottles.

Tasting notes
  • Sight: intense cherry red with a purple rim, clean and bright.
  • Nose: ripe red fruit with spice, toasted coffee and cocoa.
  • Palate: savory and fleshy, with fruit and spice — complex and full of personality.
  • Pairing: red meats and jamón serrano.
Vineyard & terroir

Continental Mediterranean climate with under 400 mm of rain a year and the cold, dry Cierzo wind. Poor, rocky soils rich in quartzite and clay. Bush-trained, dry-farmed vines over 40 years old at 2,000 vines per hectare, hand-harvested into 25 kg crates in the first two weeks of October 2023.

Winemaking

Cold pre-fermentation maceration, then fermentation at 25 °C with native yeast and a two-week maceration in 20,000 L truncated-cone tanks. Malolactic fermentation in second-use French oak, followed by eight months on lees in 300 and 500 L French barrels, a 3,000 L foudre and 3,000 L concrete spheres.
